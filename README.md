# Portable Office

Portable Office is a personal office suite that runs from a folder on your computer or on PHP hosting. It brings Mail, Calendar, Tasks, Contacts, Documents, Wiki, Sheets, and Lists into one keyboard-oriented workspace.

## Get started

- [Install Office](docs/installation.md) on Windows, Linux, or cPanel.
- [Set up your workspace](docs/getting-started.md) and create your first item.
- [Browse the user guides](docs/README.md) for each app, keyboard shortcuts, backups, updates, and more.

## Development

- [Run, build, and test a source checkout](docs/development.md).
- [Package and publish a release](docs/releases.md).
