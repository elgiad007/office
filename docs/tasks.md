# Tasks

[Office user guides](README.md)

Open **Tasks**, press `g 3`, or enter `:tasks`. Choose **New task** and enter a title and optional description, due date, and priority. Choose **Save** to create the task. Priorities are **High**, **Normal**, and **Low**. Existing tasks with a custom priority retain it.

## Organize the task list

Open **List view** above the task rows. Filter by status, priority, or due date. Group by due date, status, or priority, and sort by due date, priority, title, or last update. Select a group heading to collapse or expand it, or choose **Expand all** or **Collapse all**. Office remembers your view settings.

By default, Tasks shows unfinished work by earliest due date, with overdue items first and undated items last. If a task is missing, check the search, filters, and collapsed groups.

## Complete and reopen tasks

Use the **Completed** checkbox in the task details or press `x` in normal mode to complete the current task. To complete several, select them and use the item menu. A completed task leaves the default view, and Office advances to a visible task. To find it again, switch **Show tasks** to **Completed** or **All tasks**, then clear **Completed** in the details to reopen it.

Tasks are stored locally. You can [link](connections.md) a task to related mail or events and use [Office item exports](import-export.md) to share tasks.
