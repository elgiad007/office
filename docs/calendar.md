# Calendar

[Office user guides](README.md)

## Find events

Open **Calendar** in the sidebar, press `g 2`, or enter `:calendar`. Choose **Month** for a broad view, **Week** or **Day** for time slots, or **Agenda** for a list. Use **Today**, the previous and next controls, or the mini calendar to change the date. In Month view, select **+ more** to see the rest of a busy day.

Use **Calendars** to show or hide local events and individual account calendars. Search from the Calendar view to find events, or choose the clock in the Office header to open **Upcoming agenda** and jump to an event.

## Create an event

Choose **New event**, select a date number, or select a time slot in Week or Day view. Enter a title, start and end, and optionally a location and description. Save the event to add it to your local calendar.

Timed events use your browser's local time when you enter them. All-day events use dates. The end date is exclusive, so for a one-day event on May 4, use May 4 as the start and May 5 as the end.

## Import, export, and sync

Use **Import** for an `.ics` file and **Export** to share events as ICS. When the original source was retained, **Download original ICS** in the event details preserves it, including recurrence information.

Google Calendar and CalDAV events appear as read-only cached copies. Sync covers the previous week through the next 90 days, up to 100 events. Google supplies expanded instances. Office shows recurrence information for imported and CalDAV series without generating extra occurrences locally. Manage invitations, recurring series edits, and reminders in your calendar provider.

See [Accounts and sync](accounts-and-sync.md) to connect a calendar.
