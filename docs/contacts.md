# Contacts

[Office user guides](README.md)

Open **Contacts**, press `g 4`, or enter `:contacts`. Choose **New contact**, enter the person's name and any email address, phone number, company, group, or notes, then choose **Save**. Search in Contacts to find a saved entry and select it to review or update its details.

Use **Import** to add contacts from a vCard address book (`.vcf`). Use **Export** to download a contact, or export multiple contacts from the selection menu. Contacts live in your Office workspace. Connecting a mail account leaves its provider address book separate.

To associate a contact with a task, message, or other record, use [Connections](connections.md).
