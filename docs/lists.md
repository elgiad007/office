# Lists

[Office user guides](README.md)

Open **Lists**, press `g 8`, or enter `:lists`. Choose **New list**, enter a name, and choose **Save**. You can also start a named checklist with `:new list Shopping`.

## Work through a checklist

- Enter text in **Add an item…** and press Enter or choose **Add item**. Paste several lines to create one item per nonblank line.
- Select a checkbox to mark an item done or reopen it. The list shows how many items are checked.
- Use **Edit** or **Remove** on a checklist row to change its wording or remove it. Use **Rename** to change the list name.
- Search in Lists to find matching list names or checklist text, including checked items.

In the checklist pane, press `o` to focus the add field and Escape to return to the checklist. With a row focused, use `j` and `k` or Up and Down to navigate, `x` or Space to toggle its checkbox, `i` to edit it, and `D` to remove it after confirmation. With the named list focused in the list pane, `i` renames it and `D` deletes the entire list after confirmation.

Checklist entries stay with their list in Office exports and backups. The named list can have [Connections](connections.md) to other Office items.
